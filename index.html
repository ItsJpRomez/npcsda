Who we are

A family of faith in Napocor Village

Since 1998, NPC Seventh-day Adventist Church has been a place where neighbors in Pasong Tamo come to study God's Word, worship together on the Sabbath, and serve our community with open hands.

Whether you've been an Adventist all your life or you're just curious about faith, you belong here.

Our Mission

To make disciples of Jesus Christ who live as His loving witnesses and share the everlasting gospel with everyone, in preparation for His soon return.

Our Vision

A Christ-centered, Bible-believing church family that is a light to Quezon City through worship, fellowship, health, and service.

Seventh-day Adventist identity

  • Seventh-day We keep the Bible Sabbath, from Friday sunset to Saturday sunset, as a day of rest and worship.
  • Adventist We joyfully await the Second Coming (the "advent") of Jesus Christ.
  • Whole-person faith We believe God cares for body, mind, and spirit, so we value health, education, and service.
  • Worldwide family We belong to a global church of more than 22 million members in over 200 countries.

Our Ministries

There's a place for every age and every gift.

  • Youth Ministry

    Adventist Youth programs, Bible study, and outreach for young people.

  • Pathfinders

    Ages 10–15. Camping, honors, drills, and character building.

  • Adventurers

    Ages 4–9. Fun, faith-filled activities for little ones and their families.

  • Women's Ministry

    Fellowship, prayer, and support for women in every season of life.

  • Music Ministry

    Choir, praise team, and special music for worship.

  • Health Ministry

    Health talks, cooking classes, and wellness programs for our community.

  • Personal Ministries

    Bible studies, evangelism, and sharing hope door to door.

  • Family & Community

    Family life seminars, feeding programs, and disaster relief.
